Internal meeting guide

A clean walkthrough for Thursday’s ABA story.

This page is for the meeting flow itself. It is not a public page. It helps us present the prototypes in a logical order, keep the narrative tight, and show how membership, knowledge, and registration visibility fit together.

Meeting objective

Show that ABA is not only an association idea. It is a coordinated platform with a public front door, a formal membership path, a knowledge layer, and a route toward stronger registration visibility and sector intelligence.

Presentation order

Move from public proposition into practical proof.

01

Start with the ABA homepage

Open with a clear public-facing front door that explains what ABA is, why it matters, and where people go next.

02

Show membership types and application flow

Demonstrate who ABA is building for, how different membership lanes work, and what data gets captured in the process.

03

Introduce the Biologicals Explorer

Show how the database concept becomes a biologicals-first knowledge surface that supports credibility and public usefulness.

04

Bridge to the registration tracker

Position the tracker as the deeper intelligence layer that will later connect to the public and member-facing surfaces.

Demo flow

What each prototype should prove in the room.

Homepage

  • ABA has a coherent public-facing proposition.
  • The homepage is a front door, not the entire site.
  • Visitors can move clearly into membership, knowledge, or future tracker areas.

Membership flow

  • Membership is intentionally structured rather than one-size-fits-all.
  • The data capture flow is practical and relationship-oriented.
  • The captured information becomes useful downstream.

Knowledge hub

  • The database concept belongs inside the ABA story.
  • It is framed around biological usefulness and evidence.
  • It creates a credible public knowledge layer around the sector.

Registration tracker

  • Registration visibility becomes concrete rather than abstract.
  • Public, member, and admin value can be shown as one system.
  • The tracker deepens the proposition without needing to dominate the homepage.
Suggested close

End on the integrated proposition.

Close by reinforcing that ABA is being designed as an ecosystem platform: a credible public site, a structured route into membership, a Biologicals Explorer that makes products more legible, and a registration-intelligence layer that can turn participation into visibility and action.
