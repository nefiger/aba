African Biologicals Alliance Prototype Suite

Three separate artifacts, three different jobs.

This workspace now separates the public-facing prototype, the internal admin prototype, and the standalone flow/value proposition asset. The intention is to make it obvious what is a website experience, what is an internal tool, and what is an explanatory stakeholder artifact.

Project framing

Participation can come from across Africa. Operational activity is currently centered in South Africa. The registration tracker must tell a public advocacy story while also feeding a more useful internal intelligence view.

Front-End Prototype

Public website direction

A plausible ABA website prototype that weaves the new journeys into the existing narrative structure rather than presenting them as isolated product cards.

Shows the four audience pathways inside a believable public-site story.
Includes a clearer registration intelligence and advocacy section with soft conversion opportunities.
Keeps Africa-wide participation visible while staying honest that ABA is active in South Africa today.

Admin Prototype

Internal dashboard and records

A distinct admin concept focused on country-grouped records, registration intelligence, flagged delays, and visibility into membership, network, and subscriber growth.

Centers the registration tracker as the main intelligence surface.
Shows how country filters, stuck signals, and bottleneck groupings can work in practice.
Keeps membership, technical network, and subscriber records visible without overbuilding workflow.

Flow / Value Asset

Stakeholder explainer

A standalone asset that explains the four flows, what data each captures, what assets they create, and why registration intelligence matters to advocacy and future chapter readiness.

Useful for alignment, fundraising, advocacy, and partner conversations.
