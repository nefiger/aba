# ABA State-Dependent Copy: After-Change Versions (draft)

Status: DRAFT for Jen's approval. Written 2026-10-08 so that no public copy has to be rewritten after launch. When a state changes, only the matching block below is swapped. The proposition and voice around it stay as they are.

## 1. Membership opens

Home band
- Heading: Apply for membership.
- Text: Applications are open to manufacturers, formulators, distributors and other organisations contributing to biological agriculture in Africa. ABA reviews every application.
- Button: Start an application.

Membership page
- The section "Membership is opening in stages" becomes "How membership works".
- The four steps become: apply, complete the application, ABA reviews it, ABA confirms.

Membership interest page
- The heading "Hear when membership opens." becomes "Stay in touch with ABA."

## 2. Member names and logos

This is data-driven, not a copy swap.
- The heading "Founding members." and its sentence stay fixed.
- Organisation names and logos appear only for members whose display permission is on file.
- The block stays hidden until at least one member has permission.
- Public listing is organisation name only. Contact details stay internal.

## 3. Tracker findings published

- Label: Regulatory intelligence.
- Heading: Registration findings.
- Text: A reviewed, sourced and dated view of how biological products move through registration. Each finding shows its source and when it was last checked, and none names a person, organisation or product.
- Button: View the findings.

Before this block is used the tracker workstream must have provenance labels, last-verified dates and the separation of tracker data from member records and sales leads in place.
