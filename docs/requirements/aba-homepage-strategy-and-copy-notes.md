# ABA Homepage Strategy and Copy Notes

Last updated: 2026-06-24

This note captures the current strategic understanding behind the ABA homepage reset. It is intended as internal handoff guidance for any agent or collaborator editing ABA's public-facing pages.

## Core strategic understanding

ABA is not primarily a software product or a data tool.

ABA is a membership-based advocacy and sector-building body for independent African biologicals manufacturers and distributors. Its central role is to improve the enabling environment for local biologicals by building policy voice, regulatory engagement, sector legitimacy, harmonisation momentum, and support for local manufacturing.

The homepage should reflect that wider role. It should feel more ambitious, political, and sector-shaping than the current thin or explanatory copy.

## What the homepage must do

The homepage is a front door, not the entire ABA story.

The first screen should make three things clear:

1. Who ABA is
2. Why ABA matters
3. Where a visitor should go next

The homepage should not try to explain every part of governance, chapters, product logic, or detailed operating model in one pass.

## Audience split

### Join ABA

Audience:
- manufacturers
- distributors
- formulators
- sector actors seeking representation, influence, legitimacy, and coordinated power

Motivation:
- join a body that can shape the sector, represent independent African interests, and create stronger conditions for local biologicals to compete

Important:
- membership must stand on its own as an alliance proposition
- it should not depend on the registration path to make sense

### Track Product Registrations

Audience:
- people with active registration pain
- people with useful registration records
- product owners
- regulatory or operations leads who want better visibility into registration journeys

Motivation:
- contribute product-registration experience into a clearer sector picture
- gain value from bottleneck visibility, stronger signals, and better advocacy evidence

Important:
- this is a separate acquisition path, not just a membership feature
- homepage framing should stay value-first and public-safe
- do not import member-only framing from the tracker page into the homepage

### Explore Biologicals

Audience:
- people looking for structured knowledge about products, evidence, crop context, and regulatory context

Motivation:
- learn, browse, compare, and understand the biologicals landscape more clearly

Important:
- this path should feel concrete and useful
- it should not read like filler between the other two paths

## Relationship between the three paths

These are parallel homepage paths, not one funnel.

- Membership is the alliance and representation path.
- Product registrations is the contribution-and-visibility path.
- Biologicals is the knowledge path.

They should cross-reference one another lightly, but the homepage should not collapse them into a single story or imply that one path only exists to feed another.

## Copy rules

Write for public readers, not meeting attendees or repo collaborators.

Do:
- lead with the value ABA creates
- use clear public language
- emphasise independent African biologicals, collective voice, regulatory conditions, local manufacturing, and coordinated advocacy
- keep copy concise, direct, and believable

Do not:
- use `workspace`, `mockup`, `demo`, `prototype`, `internal material`, or similar framing on the homepage
- narrate the site itself
- write as though the page is mainly for collaborators reviewing a concept
- make the registration path sound private or member-only at first contact
- make the biologicals path sound secondary or decorative

## What membership should mean in copy

Membership is about:
- representation
- influence
- governance participation
- legitimacy
- coordinated advocacy
- sector coordination

Registration support can appear inside the membership value proposition, but it should not define membership by itself.

## What the registration path should mean in copy

On the homepage, the registration path should be framed around:
- clearer visibility into product-registration journeys
- better understanding of bottlenecks and progress
- stronger evidence for sector advocacy

Do not rewrite the tracker presenter page in this phase. Only make sure the homepage uses a cleaner public framing when linking to that path.

## What the biologicals path should mean in copy

This is the shared knowledge layer.

It should feel useful to both members and non-members by making products, evidence, and regulatory context easier to access and understand.

## Guardrails for this phase

- Do not edit `registration-tracker/`
- Do not edit [`docs/registration-tracker/index.html`](/Users/nefiger/projects/aba/docs/registration-tracker/index.html)
- Preserve `Public Sans`
- Preserve the restrained public visual language already used across the ABA public pages
- Keep Africa-wide participation and South Africa-active positioning explicit

## Practical editing reminder

If a block of homepage copy sounds like it is explaining the repo, the meeting, or the prototype, it is probably wrong for the public homepage.

If a block makes ABA sound like a generic information project rather than a sector-building alliance, it should be rewritten.
