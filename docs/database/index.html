Biologicals-first database

A biologicals knowledge layer, not a hazard side-project.

This area reframes the existing database concept so it starts with biological products, sector usefulness, and market visibility. Instead of leading with a hazard frame, it becomes a public knowledge surface that helps people understand products, categories, evidence, and regulatory context.

Explore product catalogue Back to ABA site
Why this matters

Knowledge, visibility, and credibility belong together.

The database should help ABA show that biologicals are a real and growing category, make it easier to explore what kinds of products exist, and create a stronger bridge between public knowledge, sector participation, and future registration intelligence.

How it fits ABA

This should strengthen the site story rather than sit beside it awkwardly.

Useful to the public

The first experience should help users explore biologicals in a structured way, not force them to decode an internal or inherited data model.

Useful to members

Over time, the knowledge layer can support stronger visibility for categories, products, and recurring sector questions that matter to members.

Useful to the ABA proposition

It helps ABA look like a platform with knowledge depth, not only a membership form or a tracker concept.

Ready for future integration

The regulatory-signals area can later connect to Lyle’s registration tracker without pretending that work is already complete.
