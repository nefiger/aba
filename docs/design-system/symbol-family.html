Construction system — softened
Every symbol sits on a 512×512 square artboard over the shared field-mark ground — three flowing contour lines in olive, ochre and terracotta. Each glyph above it is built from curved paths, soft arcs and rounded-end strokes: nodes are circles, connectors bow gently rather than running straight, and volumes use rounded capsules and drops in place of boxes and hard angles. The result stays geometric and restrained in its logic — but reads as grown, not engineered, in keeping with a living biological system rather than a technical grid.
