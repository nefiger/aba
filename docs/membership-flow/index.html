Member value

Why join ABA.

Representation and policy voice

ABA gives independent African biologicals participants a stronger shared presence in regulatory and policy spaces than any one organisation can carry alone.

Compliance and registration support

Members gain access to registration guidance, visibility into product pathways, and a more structured basis for support and advocacy.

Knowledge and capability

Training, briefings, templates, and biologicals intelligence help members make stronger decisions and strengthen their teams.

Credibility and collaboration

Membership helps create a more visible, better organised, and more credible sector with room for local producers, researchers, service providers, and aligned institutions.

Three routes

Choose your membership route.

Full membership

For commercial biologicals participants.

For manufacturers, product owners, importers, distributors, and smaller local producers directly active in the biologicals market.

Best for

Organisations or individuals with products, commercial biologicals activity, or direct market participation.

What matters in review

Product role, category fit, market footprint, and independence from multinational agrochemical control.

Includes

Voting membership route, stronger policy voice, and the clearest connection to registration enablement.

Apply for Full membership
Associate membership

For technical and ecosystem contributors.

For researchers, consultants, implementers, service providers, and allied specialists who strengthen the biologicals sector through expertise or enabling work.

Best for

People and organisations contributing research, trials, testing, extension, regulatory support, or cross-category technical work.

What matters in review

Expertise area, biologicals focus, country relevance, and how the applicant strengthens the alliance.

Includes

Access to ABA’s knowledge and sector work without framing every applicant as a commercial voting member.

Apply for Associate membership
Observer membership

For institutional and public-interest stakeholders.

For funders, development partners, public institutions, and related bodies that want to engage the category without being framed as core commercial members.

Best for

Institutions following biologicals sector development, programme visibility, policy shifts, or collaboration opportunities.

What matters in review

Institution type, engagement intent, country relevance, and the kind of relationship they want with ABA.

Includes

A cleaner route for observers to stay connected without being pushed into the wrong membership frame.

Apply for Observer membership
Compare the routes

Compare Full, Associate, and Observer.

Full
Who it is for

Commercial biologicals participants, including smaller local producers and product-led businesses.

Key signals captured

Product role, product category, registration relevance, market countries, ownership, and independence.

Membership position

Voting membership route with the strongest fit to ABA’s commercial and regulatory agenda.

Associate
Who it is for

Technical contributors, researchers, consultants, extension actors, service providers, and other supporting specialists.

Key signals captured

Expertise area, biologicals focus, contribution model, and country or region relevance.

Membership position

Non-voting route for people and organisations that strengthen the sector without being its commercial core.

Observer
Who it is for

Funders, public bodies, regional institutions, and other stakeholders following the sector from an institutional position.

Key signals captured

Institution type, engagement intent, sector-interest areas, and geographic relevance.

Membership position

Non-voting route that keeps institutional participation visible without confusing it with commercial membership.

Fit and eligibility

Full membership is for independent commercial participation in biologicals.

Full membership review
  • Commercial role in biologicals
  • Product and market relevance
  • African ownership and control
  • Independence from multinational agrochemical control
Smaller producers are welcome
  • Early-stage businesses can apply
  • Current production or market stage can be described clearly
  • Large portfolio size is not required
  • Small-producer fee treatment can be handled during review
