Membership value

Why join ABA.

Membership is not just a listing or a badge. It creates a stronger shared vehicle for influence, practical registration support, and a more credible operating environment for independent African biologicals.

What membership strengthens
Collective voice

Shared presence in policy, regulatory, and market discussions.

Practical support

Clearer registration pathways, support material, and participation structure.

Sector credibility

More visible, better organised positioning for local biologicals actors.

Operating principle

ABA is being built as an independent African alliance, not a neutral directory and not an extension of dominant BigAg interests.

01

Representation and policy voice

ABA gives independent African biologicals participants a stronger shared presence in regulatory and policy spaces than any one organisation can carry alone.

02

Compliance and registration support

Members gain access to registration guidance, visibility into product pathways, and a more structured basis for support and advocacy.

03

Knowledge and capability

Training, briefings, templates, and biologicals intelligence help members make stronger decisions and strengthen their teams.

04

Credibility and collaboration

Membership helps create a more visible, better organised, and more credible sector with room for local producers, researchers, service providers, and aligned institutions.

How application works.

Choose the membership route that fits your role. The application is then captured for ABA review rather than instant sign-up.

01

Choose your membership

Select Full Member, Technical Partner, or Observer based on how you participate in the sector.

02

Apply as an individual or organisation

Each route supports both applicant types.

03

Complete the relevant form

Share the product, market, expertise, geographic, and consent details relevant to your membership.

04

ABA reviews the application

ABA checks fit, follows up where needed, and confirms the next state.

Product applicant?

Full-member applicants with products can continue into product intake after the core application is captured.

What happens after you apply.

After submission, the application becomes a reviewable ABA record. Next steps are handled deliberately, with manual follow-up where needed.

01
Application received

ABA captures the submission as a reviewable record rather than granting instant membership.

02
Under review

ABA reviews role fit, independence, geography, and the best next step for the relationship.

03
Next state confirmed

ABA then confirms the right outcome instead of assuming immediate activation.

04
Manual follow-up and invoicing

This process does not assume instant payment. Any fee handling follows approval and manual ABA follow-up.

Possible next states

ABA confirms the right next move.

After review, ABA may request clarification, move a full-member applicant into product intake, approve the application, hold it pending a next action, or decline it.

  • More information required
  • Invited into product intake
  • Approved
  • Held pending next action
  • Declined
Review model

All applications are reviewed by ABA. No instant membership is granted, and invoicing happens only after the relevant follow-up.
