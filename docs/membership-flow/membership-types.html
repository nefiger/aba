Membership Types

Which option is right for you?

For industry participants

Full Member

Full membership is intended for organisations working directly in the biologicals market, including manufacturers, product owners, importers, and distributors.

Best if biological products are central to your organisation’s work
Read full details
+

Full membership is for organisations whose day-to-day work is directly tied to biological products and the market around them. This may include organisations with products already in market, organisations moving products through registration, or organisations preparing to commercialise biologicals more actively.

This may be right for you if
  • Your organisation manufactures, imports, distributes, or owns biological products
  • Your products are already in market, in registration, or moving toward registration
  • You want a more active formal relationship with ABA
What this is intended to support

Full membership is the clearest fit for organisations who want to be part of ABA’s core industry network and whose work is directly affected by how the biologicals sector develops.

For ecosystem contributors

Associate Member

Associate membership is intended for organisations contributing to the sector through research, technical work, programmes, implementation, or specialist support.

Best if you support the biologicals sector without operating as a core industry member
Read full details
+

Associate membership is for organisations that help strengthen the biologicals sector without sitting in the same position as core industry participants. This may include research institutions, NGOs, technical partners, service organisations, and aligned programmes.

This may be right for you if
  • Your organisation contributes research, technical support, programme work, training, or specialist capacity
  • You want to be part of the biologicals ecosystem without applying as a core industry member
  • Your relationship with ABA is likely to be collaborative, supportive, or knowledge-based
What this is intended to support

Associate membership helps ABA stay connected to the wider network of organisations that contribute to sector growth, capability, and alignment.

For institutional stakeholders

Observer

Observer status is intended for public institutions, funders, development partners, and other strategic stakeholders who want a formal relationship with ABA.

Best if your role is institutional, strategic, or public-facing rather than operational
Read full details
+

Observer status is for institutions that should be in formal relationship with ABA, but are not joining in the same way as industry or ecosystem members. This may include government stakeholders, development institutions, and funding partners.

This may be right for you if
  • You represent a public institution, funder, development partner, or similar strategic stakeholder
  • You want a formal route into the ABA network without entering the same path as operational members
  • Your relationship with ABA is likely to be institutional, strategic, or public-facing
What this is intended to support

Observer status provides a formal way for ABA to stay connected to important institutional relationships while keeping the distinction between member participation and stakeholder alignment clear.

If you are applying as a manufacturer, importer, distributor, or product owner, start with Full Membership. If your organisation’s role is primarily research, technical support, programme work, or sector strengthening, Associate Membership is usually a better fit. If you represent a public institution, funder, or similar stakeholder, Observer status is likely to be the most appropriate place to begin.

What To Expect

What joining ABA is intended to help with.

A clearer relationship with ABA

Membership gives ABA a clearer understanding of who your organisation is, how it fits into the sector, and what kind of relationship or follow-up may be most useful.

Better visibility across the sector

Membership helps ABA build a stronger picture of who is active in biologicals, in which countries, and with what kinds of roles, capabilities, or institutional responsibilities.

More relevant follow-up

The application gives ABA more than contact details. It helps the team understand what kind of conversation, support, or next step is likely to make sense for your organisation.

A stronger sector platform over time

For organisations in the core industry lane especially, ABA is working toward a stronger platform for sector visibility, coordination, and practical support as the network matures.

ABA membership is not a guarantee of approvals, market outcomes, or institutional access. It is the beginning of a more formal relationship with ABA and a clearer place for your organisation within the broader biologicals sector network.
