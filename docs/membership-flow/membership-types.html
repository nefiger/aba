Quick rule

If you are commercially active in biologicals, start with Full member. If you strengthen the sector through expertise or enabling work, start with Technical partner. If you are an institutional stakeholder, start with Observer.

Full

Commercial route

For product owners, manufacturers, distributors, importers, and smaller local producers active in biologicals.

  • Voting membership route
  • Strongest connection to registration and market-access support
  • Requires eligibility and independence review
  • Can continue into product intake where relevant
Apply
Technical partner

Technical route

For researchers, consultants, specialists, service providers, implementers, and allied ecosystem contributors.

  • Non-voting route
  • Captures expertise, biologicals focus, and contribution model
  • Designed for people and organisations strengthening sector capability
Apply
Observer

Institutional route

For funders, development partners, public institutions, and other sector stakeholders engaging from an institutional position.

  • Non-voting route
  • Captures engagement intent and sector-interest areas
  • Creates a cleaner place for institutional participation
