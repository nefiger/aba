Membership types

Choose the membership option that fits your organisation.

ABA offers different membership routes for commercial participants, technical contributors, and institutional stakeholders.

At a glance

Full for commercial participants, Associate for technical contributors, Observer for institutional stakeholders.

Membership detail

Compare the available membership routes.

Full membership

For commercial biologicals participants

+

This route is intended for manufacturers, importers, distributors, and product owners who are directly active in the biologicals market.

  • Best for organisations with direct commercial activity in biologicals.
  • Suitable for businesses with products, market presence, or commercial operations.
  • Designed for organisations playing a central role in the market.
Associate membership

For technical and ecosystem contributors

+

This route is intended for organisations that strengthen the sector through research, field implementation, specialist services, technical support, or adjacent ecosystem work.

  • Suitable for contributors whose role is technical, specialist, or supportive.
  • Creates space for expertise, services, and implementation support.
  • Helps keep technical capacity visible within the alliance.
Observer status

For institutional and public-interest stakeholders

+

This route is intended for funders, development partners, public institutions, and similar stakeholders who want to engage the sector without being framed as core commercial members.

  • Suitable for organisations that want to stay connected to the sector.
  • Supports engagement without treating every participant as a commercial member.
  • Creates room for wider institutional participation.
