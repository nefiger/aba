About the Alliance

Built by manufacturers. For manufacturers.

The African Biologicals Alliance is a membership-based sector organisation of independent biological and alternative input manufacturers and distributors. We exist to strengthen the conditions under which safer, locally produced inputs can compete fairly in African agriculture.

We provide a collective voice in policy and regulatory spaces, work to improve registration pathways and market conditions, and champion local manufacturing as an economic and environmental priority.

Working truth

“Transitioning away from extractive, multinational monopoly business models is as important as transitioning away from the hazardous pesticides they push into African markets.”

Why this exists

African agriculture faces a structural problem.

The conditions that favour hazardous pesticide imports over safer local alternatives are systemic, and fixing them requires organised, collective action.
