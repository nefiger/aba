African Biologicals Alliance

Biologicals in Africa deserve a stronger foundation.

The biological crop protection sector in Africa is growing — but it is fragmented, under-represented, and navigating regulatory environments built for a different era. ABA exists to change that: a shared home for the people and organisations working to make biologicals a credible, connected part of African agriculture.

Who this is for

Manufacturers, importers, distributors, researchers, service providers, and institutions working in biologicals across Africa.

ABA is building from South Africa and expanding across the continent. Founding members shape what that looks like.

Why ABA

The sector has the products. It needs the infrastructure.

Registration is slow and opaque

Biological products face regulatory pathways designed for conventional chemistry. Members navigating those pathways are doing it alone. ABA creates a shared record layer and a credible voice with the Registrar's office.

Knowledge is scattered

Trials, product data, MRLs, and sector intelligence exist — but not in one place. The Biologicals Explorer makes that knowledge accessible, structured, and usable for the people who need it.

The sector speaks with many voices

Individual companies, researchers, and institutions each engage the system separately. ABA gives the sector a shared presence: a consistent identity, aggregated evidence, and coordinated engagement.
