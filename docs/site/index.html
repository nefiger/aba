African Biologicals Alliance

A stronger voice for biologicals in Africa.

The African Biologicals Alliance brings together the people and organisations working to grow, support, and strengthen the biologicals sector across the continent.

Become a member Explore biologicals
Who this is for

Companies, technical experts, researchers, partners, and institutions working in biologicals.

ABA welcomes participation from across Africa while building from its current base in South Africa.

Why ABA

Biologicals need visibility, connection, and support.

Make the sector easier to understand

Biologicals should be easier to explore, explain, and discuss with confidence.

Bring the right people together

Industry participants, technical contributors, and institutional stakeholders all have a role to play.

Support better sector engagement

Stronger information and clearer participation can support better conversation, coordination, and action.

Registration visibility

Registration visibility is one way ABA turns member participation into sector intelligence.

Members do not just submit records into a black box. ABA can help organise what is waiting, what evidence is ready, and what patterns matter for sector advocacy.
