How ABA handles privacy, participation, and member-facing obligations.

These pages outline the policy surfaces the prototype already needs: public privacy and POPIA handling, general site terms, member-specific use conditions, and route-specific data consent expectations.

ABA already captures membership, update, and registration-intelligence inputs. Formal legal drafting still needs board and counsel review, but the prototype now treats these policy areas as required product surfaces, not optional afterthoughts.

Privacy & POPIA

Privacy and POPIA notice

ABA needs a clear public notice covering personal and organisational information captured through membership, updates, and registration-intelligence routes.

  • What information ABA collects
  • Why ABA collects it
  • How long it is kept
  • Who inside ABA may access it
  • How a person requests correction or removal where appropriate
Terms of use

Public website terms

The public site needs general terms for browsing, contributing information, and using ABA knowledge surfaces responsibly.

  • Permitted use of the site and public resources
  • No misuse of tracker or form submissions
  • No implied regulatory or commercial guarantee from public information
  • Appropriate attribution and reuse expectations
Member terms

Member-specific conditions

Membership introduces additional obligations beyond ordinary public usage. These terms need to cover conduct, access, confidentiality, and member-only resources.

  • Code of ethics and independence expectations
  • Use of member-only materials and operational data
  • Participation boundaries for technical partners and observers
  • Grounds for review, suspension, or withdrawal where relevant
Data & consent

Route-specific data handling

The prototype already implies different consent expectations across membership, updates, and tracker routes. Those should be made explicit and legible.

  • Membership review and follow-up consent
  • Newsletter and update permissions
  • Use of aggregate signals for advocacy and sector intelligence
  • Links between membership records and future tracker submissions
