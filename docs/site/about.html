About ABA

ABA exists to support and strengthen the biologicals sector in Africa.

The African Biologicals Alliance brings together organisations, specialists, and partners who are working to grow the reach, credibility, and contribution of biologicals across the continent.

What ABA does

ABA connects people, knowledge, and shared sector interests.

It creates a place for participation, visibility, and practical collaboration.

Role

Provide a clearer structure for how the biologicals sector connects, participates, and presents itself.

Need

Support stronger visibility, better coordination, and more informed engagement.

Scope

Work across Africa while building from current activity in South Africa.

How ABA helps

Support for participation, knowledge, and shared progress.

Membership that fits different roles

Companies, technical contributors, and institutional stakeholders can each join in a way that makes sense for their role.

Clearer sector visibility

A better picture of products, organisations, and participation helps the sector become easier to understand.

Member-specific advocacy

Members can bring product, evidence, and registration context into a structured workspace so ABA can understand where support is needed.

Sector-wide advocacy

Aggregated signals help ABA speak about shared bottlenecks and opportunities without exposing sensitive member-level detail.

Trusted review layer

ABA review keeps records useful, credible, and ready for the right audience before they become public intelligence or advocacy evidence.
