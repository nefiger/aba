About ABA

ABA is being built to make the biologicals sector easier to organise, represent, and understand.

The alliance is intended to be a practical platform for sector participation. It should help bring together organisations, expertise, and institutional relationships in a way that feels credible, useful, and visible.

What that means

ABA is not just a list of members.

It is a structure for participation, public knowledge, and stronger sector engagement over time.

Role

Create a clearer public and sector-facing structure around biologicals participation in Africa.

Need

Support visibility, coordination, and a more grounded basis for engagement and advocacy.

Scope

Work across Africa while building from current operational activity in South Africa.

What ABA is building toward

A tighter structure for participation, knowledge, and evidence.

Membership with clearer fit

Industry participants, technical contributors, and institutional stakeholders should each have a more intentional route into ABA.

Better sector visibility

A stronger picture of products, organisations, and participation can help the sector feel more legible.

Public knowledge assets

A biologicals-first information layer can make the category easier to explore and discuss.

Evidence that can support action

Over time, clearer visibility can support more informed conversations around regulation, access, and growth.
