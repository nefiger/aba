About ABA

The biologicals sector in Africa needs a credible, connected home.

Biological crop protection and biostimulants are growing across Africa — but the sector is fragmented, its voice is thin, and the regulatory environment was not designed with it in mind. ABA is the alliance that changes that: a membership body, a knowledge resource, and a trusted intermediary between what members know and what the sector needs to say.

Building from South Africa

ABA is anchored in South Africa — where the regulatory framework, market activity, and founding membership are currently concentrated.

The model is designed to extend across Africa as participation grows. South Africa is the starting point, not the ceiling.

The problem

Companies working in biologicals are navigating registration pathways, market access questions, and credibility challenges without a shared structure to draw on. Each company carries the full weight alone.

What ABA provides

Membership, shared knowledge, structured registration intelligence, and a trusted review layer that turns what members know into what the sector can credibly say — without exposing private company detail.

The ambition

A biologicals sector in Africa that is well-represented, easier to navigate, and taken seriously by the regulatory and policy environments it operates within.

How ABA helps

Five things ABA does that the sector currently lacks.

A trusted intermediary — not just a network

ABA sits between what members know privately and what the sector can say publicly. That review layer is what makes shared intelligence usable — it protects sensitive detail while making the aggregate credible.

Registration support for members

Members can submit registration records to ABA. ABA structures them, tracks their status, and uses them to provide member-specific Registrar-office advocacy — the kind that requires organised, reviewed evidence.

Sector-wide advocacy grounded in data

Aggregated registration signals — delays, recurring bottlenecks, category-level patterns — give ABA something to say to regulators and policymakers that no individual company can say alone.

A shared knowledge base for the category

The Biologicals Explorer makes products, crop contexts, evidence records, MRLs, and regulatory standards accessible in one place — structured for the people who need to use them, not just store them.

Membership that fits different roles

Industry members, technical contributors, and institutional stakeholders each have a route into ABA. Structure is designed to be proportionate to role — not a single flat membership for everyone.
