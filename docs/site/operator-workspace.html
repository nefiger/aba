System notes

What the prototype is demonstrating

  • The public site is the invitation and framing layer.
  • Membership is a reviewable relationship, not an instant checkout.
  • Registration intake is open to authorised submitters, including non-members.
  • Member company workspaces show only company-scoped records and next actions.
  • ABA operators convert raw submissions into trusted intelligence and packet decisions.
