System logic

Raw public participation is only useful once ABA can group it by country, stage, stakeholder type, product category, expertise area, and recurring bottlenecks. That grouping layer is what turns submissions into relationship assets, sector intelligence assets, and eventually strategic action.

Registration intelligence expansion

Why the registration lane now carries the advocacy story.

Products getting stuck is not only an operational frustration. When enough people contribute registration data, ABA can show where delays cluster, how long they last, which issues recur, and what that means for biologicals progress across the continent.
