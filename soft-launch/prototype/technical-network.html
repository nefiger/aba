Technical Network

Join ABA's Technical Network.

ABA is recruiting scientists, regulatory specialists, researchers, agronomists, manufacturers and market practitioners who can support credible biologicals work across Africa.

Start the application ↘

Who should apply

People with relevant experience who share ABA's purpose and are prepared to work within its standards.

ABA reviews every application. Applying does not create ABA membership or permission to speak for ABA.

Where ABA needs expertise

Who should apply

The network will help ABA test evidence, understand country and regulatory conditions, and build practical capability with its members.

01

Evidence and research

Biology, toxicology, trials, laboratories, data quality and evidence review.

02

Regulation and policy

Registration requirements, dossier preparation, public policy and regulator-facing work.

03

Production and field use

Formulation, manufacturing, quality, agronomy, extension and biologicals in practice.

04

Markets and countries

Commercialisation, economics, training, communication and experience in African markets.

What ABA expects

How ABA assesses applicants

  • Use evidence carefully.Separate evidence, professional judgment and opinion.
  • Protect private information.Keep confidential information within its agreed use.
  • Declare competing interests.Tell ABA when a commercial or professional interest may affect the work.
  • Make responsible claims.Do not overstate products, evidence, regulatory outcomes or professional authority.
