Membership

Why join ABA?

Membership gives biologicals organisations a direct role in the work the sector needs to do together.

Express membership interest ↗
Who membership is for

Manufacturers, formulators, distributors and other organisations contributing to biological agriculture in Africa. Membership is reviewed against ABA's approved criteria.

What membership offers

What members do through ABA.

Members bring real priorities, evidence and expertise. ABA coordinates the work that is stronger when the sector does it together.

01

Collective representation

Help decide which shared issues ABA takes into regulatory, policy and market discussions.

02

Better regulatory evidence

Turn real registration experience into a clearer account of where requirements do not fit biological products.

03

Product and sector credibility

Contribute accurate, reviewed information that helps others understand biological products and the sector behind them.

04

Technical capability

Connect scientific, regulatory, manufacturing and market knowledge that no organisation holds alone.

05

Member decisions

Take part in agreed priorities, working groups and governance as ABA's structures are established.

06

Sector development

Help shape practical work on local manufacturing, skills, market access and responsible growth.

How membership opens

Membership is opening in stages.

Founding members complete the application first. Later cohorts use the same page, and ABA may make it public when ready.

  1. 01

    Express interest

    Leave your details so ABA can contact you when an appropriate membership group opens.

  2. 02

    Apply when applications open

    ABA shares the same member application with each group as it opens.

  3. 03

    Complete the application

    Tell ABA about the organisation, its work and how it wants to take part.

  4. 04

    ABA reviews it

    ABA may ask questions before confirming whether the organisation can join.

Membership boundaries

Membership does not guarantee faster registration, a regulatory decision, product endorsement, referrals or sales.
