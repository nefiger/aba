Registration Tracker

Track a registration.

Use this when a biological product is being prepared for or moving through registration. Tell ABA where it stands, what has happened and what is holding it up.

How the tracker works ↓

Before you begin

Share where the registration stands.

  • Use the tracker when

    A biological product is being prepared for, submitted or moving through registration.

  • Membership

    You do not need to be an ABA member.

  • What to have ready

    The application, its current status, work already completed and the problem that needs attention.

Who can use it

You do not need to be a member.

Anyone responsible for a biological product registration can use the tracker.

Tell ABA where the process stands and what is holding it up. You will not be asked to upload a full confidential dossier.

Prepare the registration details.

Have the product, application status, work completed and the issue that needs attention ready before you start.

How ABA may use the information

You control what ABA shares.

You decide whether ABA may use the information without naming your organisation or product. Any named use requires separate permission.

Kept private

Your submission

Your organisation, product and application details are not published by default.

Used without names

Shared patterns

With the right permission, ABA may combine reviewed information to show common delays or barriers without naming a company or product.

Used with names

Only with permission

ABA needs separate permission and review before using your organisation, product or registration matter by name.

What the public will see

ABA publishes reviewed information only.

ABA will only publish a sector view when there is enough real information and the required permissions are in place. Nothing is published simply because it was submitted.

Using the tracker does not make you a member and does not sign you up for unrelated ABA emails.
