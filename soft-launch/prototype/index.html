African Biologicals Alliance

Building Africa's biologicals sector.

African manufacturers, formulators, distributors and specialists are building an organised sector with a stronger voice, more suitable regulation, clearer product information and better conditions for local biologicals.

Why ABA exists

Common problems need a common voice.

Biologicals companies across Africa face many of the same problems, but usually have to tackle them alone.

Today

Registration requirements are often unclear or poorly suited to biologicals. Local products and expertise can be hard to find, and one company has limited influence on its own.

What ABA does

ABA identifies problems shared across the sector, gathers the facts behind them and takes agreed priorities into regulatory, policy and market discussions.

Why membership matters

Members decide what matters.

Members bring the issues they face in practice. Together, they decide where ABA should focus and what the alliance should take forward.
