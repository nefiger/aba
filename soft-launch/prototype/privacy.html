Privacy and data use

Privacy and data use.

ABA uses information for the purpose stated on each form. The permissions and acknowledgements for one form do not automatically apply to another.

Public forms

Why ABA asks for information.

What you submit on one form stays with that form. Filling in one does not also submit another application, grant another permission, or change your membership.

Form or actionHow ABA uses the information
Membership interestABA uses the contact details and separate email permission to communicate about membership opening and relevant ABA updates. This is not a membership application.
Member applicationABA uses the submission to review or confirm the applicant’s organisation and membership information. Member organisations may be named in ABA’s public member list; individual contact details are not published. General ABA updates require a separate choice.
Technical NetworkABA reviews the professional details, expertise, and declarations. Acceptance and any later public profile are separate decisions.
Registration TrackerABA uses the raw submission for follow-up and review. Approved information may contribute to grouped registration insights that do not name participants, under the required tracker acknowledgement.

Registration Tracker

How ABA protects tracker information.

Information collected
Your contact and organisation details, product and registration information, current status, submission confirmations, pathway-fit answer and permission choice.
Why ABA may contact you
ABA checks whether the information is clear enough to use and may ask you to confirm or correct a detail.
Permission to use grouped information
To use the tracker, you must agree that ABA may include suitable, non-identifying information in grouped registration insights.
What public insights do not identify
Public insights do not publish your contact details or name you, your organisation or your product.
Pathway-fit answers
ABA can read your answer and private note. Public insights may show grouped answers, but private notes are never published.
Membership and unrelated communications
Using the tracker does not create, approve, or change ABA membership and does not give ABA permission to send unrelated general updates.
What the tracker does not do
The tracker does not submit an application to the registrar, provide legal or regulatory advice, or make your registration publicly searchable.

Questions and corrections

Questions or corrections.

Reply to the ABA invitation or submission email to ask a question, correct a submission, or stop optional communications.
