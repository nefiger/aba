Privacy and data use

Privacy and data use.

ABA uses information for the purpose stated on each form. The permissions and acknowledgements for one form do not automatically apply to another.

Public forms

Why ABA asks for information.

What you submit on one form stays with that form. Filling in one does not also submit another application, grant another permission, or change your membership.

Form or actionHow ABA uses the information
Membership interestABA uses the contact details and separate email permission to communicate about membership opening and relevant ABA updates. This is not a membership application.
Member applicationABA uses the submission to review or confirm the applicant’s organisation and membership information. General ABA updates require a separate choice.
Technical NetworkABA reviews the professional details, expertise, and declarations. Acceptance and any later public profile are separate decisions.
Registration TrackerABA uses the raw submission for follow-up and review. Approved information may contribute to grouped registration insights that do not name participants, under the required tracker acknowledgement.

Registration Tracker

What the tracker collects and how it may be used.

Information collected
Contact, organisation, product, registration type, current status and date, submission confirmations, professional accountability, and acknowledgement information.
ABA review and follow-up
ABA uses the raw submission to assess its completeness and quality, ask for clarification, and select one review outcome: Approved for insights, Needs clarification, or Excluded.
Why this is required
Submitting through the tracker requires acknowledgement that reviewed information may be used in grouped registration insights that do not name participants. This is a condition of using the tracker, not an optional public-use choice.
What the public insights view does not identify
It does not publish the participant’s contact details or name the participant, organisation, or product. A submission does not become an automatic public record or immediate finding.
Membership and unrelated communications
Using the tracker does not create, approve, or change ABA membership and does not give ABA permission to send unrelated general updates.
Current tracker limits
The current tracker does not provide participant accounts, saved drafts, post-submission editing, automated publication, or a live registrar export. ABA does not give legal or regulatory advice through the tracker.

Questions and corrections

Ask ABA to explain or correct information.

Reply to the ABA invitation or submission email to ask a question, request a correction, or stop optional communications. Tracker corrections are handled by ABA; there is no participant editing account.
