Privacy and data use

Your information.

ABA uses the information you submit for the reason stated on that form. Private information is not published by default.

In plain language

Why ABA asks.

Each form explains what ABA needs, what it will do with the information and whether it may contact you afterwards.

  • Membership interest

    ABA uses your contact details to email you about membership opening and relevant alliance news. This is not a membership application.

  • Private membership application

    ABA uses the information to assess or confirm your organisation's membership. You choose separately whether to receive general ABA news.

  • Technical Network

    ABA uses your professional details and declarations to review your application. It will not publish a profile without a separate decision and permission.

  • Registration Tracker

    ABA uses tracker information to understand and follow up on the registration you submit. It does not make you a member or sign you up for unrelated emails.

Your choices

Make a change.

You can ask ABA to correct your information, stop optional emails or explain how it is using your information.

ABA does not publish by default

Contact details, applications, product information and tracker submissions remain private unless ABA has a clear reason, completes its review and has the required permission.

How to contact ABA

Reply to your ABA invitation or submission email if you want to correct information, stop optional emails or ask how your information is being used.
