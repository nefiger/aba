# Soft-Launch Content and Claims Matrix

Status: initial Phase 1 matrix
Last updated: 2026-07-20

## Purpose

Keep the membership value proposition strong and specific while preventing the website from promising mechanisms, outcomes, or services ABA cannot yet reliably deliver.

`Available now` describes what the soft launch can honestly support. `Direction to explain carefully` can appear as bounded organisational intent. `Do not promise` is prohibited without a later decision, operating model, and evidence.

## Core organisational proposition

| Topic | Publicly usable claim | Status/source basis | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| What ABA is | A membership-based African biologicals sector body working to strengthen regulatory pathways, locally rooted participation, sector coordination, and a coherent voice. | Brochure and strategy baseline | Do not imply a complete continental chapter network. |
| Geographic scope | ABA is based in South Africa and welcomes participation from across Africa. | Accepted | Do not imply active operations or representation in countries where ABA has none. |
| Why ABA exists | Biologicals need clearer pathways, credible sector evidence, coordinated representation, technical capability, and stronger conditions for local participation. | Brochure, transcripts, WhatsApp synthesis | Avoid antagonistic or unverified claims about regulators or other bodies. |
| Practical stance | ABA is solutions-led: better biological readiness and transition conditions, grounded in efficacy, safety, regulatory, and farmer realities. | Strategic evidence | Do not promise immediate replacement of all chemical inputs. |

## Membership value proposition

| Value area | What the release may say | Available mechanism at soft launch | Do not promise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collective representation | Members participate in a more organised voice for the biologicals sector and help shape priorities for constructive engagement. | Controlled membership cohort; structured collection of member context and priorities; ABA-led engagement | Personal access to the registrar, preferential treatment, faster approvals, or guaranteed outcomes |
| Regulatory evidence and registration visibility | ABA is building a clearer evidence base about real registration journeys and barriers so sector engagement can be better informed. | Lyle's tracker for actual registration situations; reviewed and consent-aware use | Full dossier handling, automatic escalation, confidential data publication, or fabricated aggregate insight |
| Sector legitimacy and product clarity | Membership supports a credible, locally rooted sector presence and the development of clearer, verified product information. | Member/company/product information capture and governed development of publication channels | Automatic product listing, ABA endorsement, registration status, or guaranteed visibility |
| Technical relationships | ABA is recruiting technical experts who share its vision and align with its values and code of conduct, creating a stronger base for credible sector work and member relationships. | Public reviewed Technical Network route with defined expertise, conduct, contribution, ownership, and onboarding boundaries | Automatic acceptance, unlimited advice, legal/regulatory/scientific sign-off, guaranteed referrals, or an immediate expert response to every public question |
| Market development and communication | ABA can develop collective channels that improve sector understanding and visibility for accurate, reviewed information. | Public website, updates, sector content, and future governed channels | A marketing package, leads, sales, individual product recommendations, or campaign results |
| Participation and governance | Members help shape ABA's priorities and, subject to approved membership/governance rules, participate in its development. | Founding and invited cohort feedback; governance information appropriate to the current stage | Voting rights, chapter rights, board roles, or privileges not yet approved in policy |
| Knowledge and learning | Members contribute to and benefit from a growing shared understanding of biologicals, regulation, and practical sector needs. | Website content, tracker evidence, approved updates, and later verified resources | A mature knowledge portal or advisory library if it is not live and maintained |

## Journey-specific copy rules

| Journey | Must communicate | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Public membership interest | What ABA is; why membership matters; membership is not publicly open; what contact permission is being requested; what happens next | Application submitted, membership granted, dues due, or guaranteed follow-up beyond the stated response |
| Member application | Every organisation completes the same application; ABA uses the information to review membership and keep approved member details current; tracker is separate if they have registrations in play | A different form or reduced question set for founding members; automatic acceptance; submission itself confirming or changing membership status; completing the tracker as a condition of membership |
| Registration tracker | Who should use it; what real situation is being captured; confidentiality/consent boundary; what ABA can learn or do with permitted information | Membership requirement, dossier upload by default, public exposure, individual case management promise, or fake dashboard |
| Technical Network | A call for aligned technical experts; expertise sought; expected contribution; values/code-of-conduct basis; review and response process | Accredited advisory service, automatic acceptance, guaranteed expert answer, or unbounded professional advice |

## Member-logo publication rule

Use neutral, clearly fictional placeholders in the prototype. Replace them only when ABA has the real logo file, confirmed organisation name, and permission to display it. A logo must not imply endorsement, current active membership, or a membership category that has not been confirmed.

## Evidence and publication rule

Before a factual external claim about regulation, safety, efficacy, registration, a product, a company, or market conditions is published, record:

1. source and date;
2. jurisdiction and scope;
3. whether it is official guidance, published research, member-supplied information, or ABA analysis;
4. reviewer/owner;
5. consent/publication basis where a person, company, product, or tracker record is involved;
6. review/expiry date.

## Prohibited shorthand

- `broader service package`
- `public aggregate signals`
- `canonical`, `shared spine`, or `continuity` in public copy
- `regulatory intelligence` where plain language is clearer
- `subscribe` or `subscription` for the public membership-interest route
- `Apply now` on the public membership route during the controlled release
