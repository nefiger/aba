# Wave 1 Copy Architecture

Status: implementation baseline
Last updated: 2026-07-21
Routes: Membership interest and Technical Network

This file records the copy structure approved for implementation before layout. It is governed by `PUBLIC-VOICE-AND-DESIGN-GUARDRAILS.md`, the final ABA brochure, the soft-launch PRD, the decision register, and the content-and-claims matrix.

## Membership interest

### Page sequence

1. `Membership interest`
2. `Hear when membership opens.`
3. ABA is inviting members in groups. A public visitor may leave contact details so ABA can email them when applications open to a group that may be relevant to them.
4. `Leave your details`
5. `Ask ABA to contact you.`
6. Contact fields
7. Specific permission to send membership-opening and relevant ABA updates
8. `What happens next`
9. ABA keeps the person's details and stated email permission; the person applies separately only if ABA later invites them.

### Action and state copy

- Primary action: `Send me membership updates`
- Submitting: `Saving your details…`
- Success: `ABA will email you when membership opens.`
- Duplicate: `ABA already has this email address.`
- Recoverable failure: `ABA could not save your details.`
- Unavailable: `The membership-interest form is temporarily unavailable.`
- Boundary: leaving details does not create a membership application or membership.

## Technical Network

### Page sequence

1. `Technical Network`
2. `Join ABA's Technical Network.`
3. ABA is recruiting scientists, regulatory specialists, researchers, agronomists, manufacturers and market practitioners who can support credible biologicals work across Africa.
4. `Where ABA needs expertise`
5. `ABA needs experts across the sector.`
6. Evidence and research
7. Regulation and policy
8. Production and field use
9. Markets, training and country knowledge
10. `What ABA expects`
11. `Applicants must meet ABA's standards.`
12. Separate evidence from opinion; protect confidential information; declare conflicts; avoid unsupported claims; work respectfully.
13. `Technical Network application`
14. `Apply to join the network.`
15. ABA reviews experience, contribution, values, and willingness to work within its code of conduct.

### Action and state copy

- Primary action: `Start the application`
- Submit: `Send my application`
- Submitting: `Sending your application…`
- Success: `ABA has received your application.`
- Duplicate: `ABA already has an application for this email address.`
- Recoverable failure: `ABA could not send your application.`
- Unavailable: `Technical Network applications are temporarily unavailable.`
- Boundary: applying does not create ABA membership, a public expert profile, permission to speak for ABA, or automatic acceptance.
