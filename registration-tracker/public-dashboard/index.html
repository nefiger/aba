Publication pipeline

Every published finding must pass the same evidence gates.

Submission starts a review process. It does not make information public or guarantee inclusion in a future finding.

  1. SubmittedA participant shares one registration.
  2. ReviewedABA checks quality and may request clarification.
  3. ClassifiedApproved information is mapped consistently.
  4. ProtectedNames and identifying detail stay out of the public view.
  5. PublishableThe combined evidence is sufficient and interpretable.

What is collected and why

Each information group has a defined purpose.

Information groupWhy ABA collects it
Product and registration typeTo compare like with like.
Current status and datesTo understand position and elapsed time in relation to the published timeframe.
Submission confirmationsTo confirm that the Application Form, Service Request Form, and proof of payment were submitted.
Professional accountabilityTo understand the submission context.
Data-use acknowledgementTo confirm the required condition for contributing reviewed information to combined insights.
