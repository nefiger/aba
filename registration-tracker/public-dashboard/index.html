Public boundary

Sector context

What the current signal picture is showing

These concise context blocks bring together the strongest public-facing sector patterns without creating a second destination for overlapping registration intelligence.

Use these signals as directional evidence: they show what is currently visible in reviewed submissions, not the full market.

Country visibility

South Africa leads the visible signal base, with additional activity emerging elsewhere.

Reviewed records are strongest in South Africa, while Kenya and a smaller set of other markets indicate where broader continental visibility is starting to form.

Recurring bottlenecks

Delay, proof gaps, and classification uncertainty remain the clearest friction points.

Repeated bottleneck themes show where advocacy and registrar engagement can focus first, especially when open applications remain in process for long periods.

Category activity

Biofertilisers and biopesticides are generating the strongest reviewed activity.

The current signal base suggests where participation is most active now, while lighter activity in other categories may reflect either early-stage formation or still-limited visibility.

Coverage forming or absent

Some pathways are beginning to show useful patterns, while others still remain too thin to read safely.

Where counts are low, ABA suppresses detail rather than over-claiming visibility. That helps show where coverage is forming and where more contribution is still needed.
