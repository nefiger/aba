This landing page explains the ABA registration tracker as one connected product area: public intake, company workspace, public signals, operator review, and registrar/export handling.

Public entry and cross-role review package
Who it is for Product owners, regulatory leads, consultants, and operators who need visibility into where registration work is moving or getting stuck.
How it connects The tracker remains open to authorised submitters, while Full member companies gain a private workspace and ABA operators control review, signals, and export handling.
Prototype status This is the tracker landing page for the current prototype snapshot. Individual pages remain the detailed source views for each role boundary and workflow.

Tracker paths

Five role-specific paths make the tracker legible as one product, not a loose set of screens.

Open flow intelligence asset
01 Submit/update registration data Authorised company representatives provide Africa-wide signals for ABA review. 02 Company workspace Verified Full member companies track their own records, blockers, and packet readiness. 03 Public evidence dashboard Reviewed records become anonymised advocacy evidence and regulatory signals. 04 Operator review ABA verifies membership, proof, consent, bottlenecks, and inclusion decisions. 05 Registrar export Reviewed, consented Full member records become controlled packet candidates.
