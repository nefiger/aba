# Registrar List Brief V1

## Audience

ABA operators preparing named, verifiable evidence packets for engagement with the registrar.

## Purpose

Show the current export-ready registrar packet list and blocked candidates, while making the export packet/batch concept explicit.

## Access / Role

Operator export workspace. Default focus is reviewed, consented, operator-approved ABA member records.

## Key Screens Or Sections

- Export-ready packet list.
- Member/non-member indicators.
- Proof and reference columns.
- Eligibility blockers.
- Export packet/batch header.
- Packet version, generated date, generated by, and packet status.
- Blocked candidates.
- Registrar engagement disclaimer.

## Important States

- Export-ready record.
- Export blocked because proof is missing.
- Export blocked because named registrar consent is missing.
- Export blocked because membership is not verified.
- Packet draft, sent, revised, or withdrawn.

## Data Shown

Fictional export rows, proof/reference statuses, eligibility blockers, packet metadata, member indicators, and batch status. Inclusion rules come from `../context-and-decisions-v1.md`.

## Relationship To CRM

The list should eventually export filtered packets from reviewed CRM-backed records and preserve packet versions, generated dates, included records, attachments, and status.

## Relationship To Other Tracker Areas

Only reviewed and operator-approved records from `admin-operator-review` should enter the export-ready list. Intake consent, company membership, and proof/reference fields determine readiness.

## Notes For Later Production Implementation

- Default packets to verified member records only.
- Non-member submissions may appear as excluded or follow-up candidates, not included by default.
- Preserve packet history as evidence sent at a moment in time.
- Prototype markup is disposable; durable rules live in `../context-and-decisions-v1.md`.
