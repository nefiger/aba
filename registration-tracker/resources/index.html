Before you submit a new registration.

What a South African Act 36 new-registration submission needs before you use the Registration Tracker. The Tracker itself only starts once the Application Form, Service Request Form, and proof of payment have all been submitted — this page is for the steps before that point.

What the registrar needs

Two forms, plus proof of payment.

RequirementWhat it is
Application FormThe formal application for the specific product and registration type being submitted.
Service Request FormCovers the requested service and product details alongside the Application Form.
Proof of paymentEvidence that the applicable registration fee has been paid.

These are the same three items the Registration Tracker asks you to confirm before you share a registration's status. It doesn't ask you to upload them — only that they've been submitted.

Biological reference sample

A sample deposit is required — the exact process is still being confirmed.

Registration requires depositing a sample of the relevant bacteria or fungi at a recognized repository. Manufacturers preparing a submission have reported checking both the Agricultural Research Council (ARC) and the South African National Biodiversity Institute (SANBI, Kirstenbosch) — neither has consistently confirmed the correct current process when contacted directly.

ABA is actively following up on this with the regulator. This page will be updated with a confirmed process as soon as that's resolved. If you've found a working answer to this yourself, that's exactly the kind of detail ABA wants to hear about.

Common delays

Most preparation delays come from three places.

  1. 01

    The Service Request Form or Application Form submitted incomplete or with mismatched product details.

  2. 02

    Proof of payment not yet reflected or not included with the submission.

  3. 03

    The biological reference sample deposit not yet resolved — see above.
