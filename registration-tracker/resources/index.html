Prepare a registration.

Check the forms, payment and biological sample requirements before sharing an application with ABA.

What the registrar needs

Two forms, plus proof of payment.

RequirementWhat it is
Application FormThe formal application for the specific product and registration type being submitted.
Service Request FormCovers the requested service and product details alongside the Application Form.
Proof of paymentEvidence that the applicable registration fee has been paid.

You will confirm these three items when you share the registration with ABA.

What you can share

Use the tracker for a new application.

You can share a new Agricultural remedy or Fertilizer application after submitting it to the registrar.

Reinstating a lapsed registration? Do not use this form. The tracker does not currently collect reinstatements.

Biological reference sample

Confirm the repository before depositing a biological sample.

A registration may require a sample of the relevant bacteria or fungi at a recognised repository. Manufacturers have reported uncertainty between the Agricultural Research Council and the South African National Biodiversity Institute at Kirstenbosch.

ABA is seeking confirmation from the regulator. Check the current requirement before sending a sample.

Final check

Resolve these three issues before submitting.

  1. 01

    Make sure the Application Form and Service Request Form are complete and contain the same product details.

  2. 02

    Include proof that the applicable registration fee has been paid.

  3. 03

    Confirm whether a biological reference sample is required and where it must be deposited.
