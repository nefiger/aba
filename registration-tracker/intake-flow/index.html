Registration tracker

Tell ABA about a product registration.

Share where a biological product registration stands, what has already been submitted, and where progress is slowing. This route is open to responsible submitters whether or not membership is already in place.

Start this route Tracker overview

Eight-step capture covering your details, product and pathway, application type, status and stage, reference and readiness, optional accountability, consent, and final submission.

Step 1. Your details

Tell ABA who is submitting, which company or organisation is involved, and how to reach you again. This information supports ABA review, anonymised sector evidence, and only with your consent any named or registrar-facing follow-up.

 

Step 2. Product, regime and legal pathway

Describe the product, the market context, and the pathway the application is moving through.

 

Some biostimulant products follow different routes depending on the claim being made. If you are unsure, choose the closest fit and ABA can follow up if needed.

Step 3. Service / application type

Select the type that best matches the work already underway. If you are unsure, choose the closest fit and ABA can clarify it during follow-up.

 

An Act 36 registration is valid for three years and must be renewed before 31 May.

Show where the application currently sits and when it reached that stage. Use the closest status if the record is still moving.
